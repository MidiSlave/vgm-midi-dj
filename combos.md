## AFX Mario · polyrhythmic two-up

Two Super Mario 64 tracks layered with a tempo gulf and a tight loop —
the kind of chaotic-but-musical mash Aphex Twin lives for.

| Slot | Track | Game | Tempo | Key | Meter | Fold | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deck 1 | `M64endin` | mario | 110 BPM | C△ | 4/4 | 1× | A |
| Deck 2 | `M64docks` | mario | 130 BPM | G△ | 4/4 | 1× | B |

- **Mix**: A+B (both audible)
- **Deck 2**: 2-bar loop engaged, playing into the gap left by deck 1
- **Why it works**: 20 BPM gulf creates a slow phase-pull between the
  decks; the loop on deck 2 keeps a stable anchor while deck 1 drifts
  around it. C△ over G△ = perfect fifth pivot, dissonance lives in
  the rhythm not the harmony.
- **Live notes**: kill BS2 + Juno on deck 1 so the bass comes only from
  deck 2's looped section. Mute deck 2's keytar so deck 1's keytar is
  the dominant lead voice.
