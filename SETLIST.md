# VGM Life Drawing Setlists

Two 45-minute sets. Pacing follows life drawing rhythm: short energetic bursts
for gesture poses, longer atmospheric pieces for sustained poses.

Key: **bold** = crowd-pleaser / instant recognition

## DJ Notes

- Most MIDI tracks are short (1-3 min) — **loop them** or crossfade from Deck A→B
- For long poses (10+ min): stack 2-3 atmospheric tracks (Aquatic Ambiance → Aerith → Brinstar)
- The Juno 106 pad + Bass Station combo will carry the atmospheric sections
- TR-08 shines on: Contra, Streets of Rage, Mega Man, Street Fighter
- SH-01A leads will cut through on: Castlevania, Sonic, Mega Man
- **Crossfade strategy**: load next track on idle deck during longer tracks,
  bring up fader when current track loops back to intro
